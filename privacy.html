Privacy Policy

One Map a Day · Last updated: October 31, 2026

This policy explains how the "One Map a Day" application ("the app") handles data. The channel owner uses the app to publish its own content to its own YouTube channels.

What the app accesses

The app uses the YouTube Data API with the scopes youtube.upload and youtube.force-ssl. It uses them only to upload videos, set thumbnails, add video metadata (titles, descriptions, scheduled publish times) and upload subtitle files to YouTube channels owned by the channel owner.

What the app does not do

  • It does not read, collect or store data about viewers or other YouTube users.
  • It does not sell, share or transfer any Google user data to third parties.
  • It does not use Google user data for advertising, analytics or AI model training.

Storage of credentials

The OAuth tokens that authorize the app are stored only as encrypted secrets in the owner's private automation environment. They are never published. You can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Use of YouTube is also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
