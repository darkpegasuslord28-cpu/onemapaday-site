🗺️ One Map a Day

One weird map, every day.

One Map a Day is an animated YouTube channel. Each day we tell one surprising story about the world with a map and a short cartoon skit. Longer episodes follow three friends: Zib the alien, Prof. Whiskers the cat and Coo the pigeon. Together they try to survive one day in a different place and time in history.

About this app

The "One Map a Day" app is an internal publishing tool used only by the channel owner. It uploads our own videos, thumbnails and subtitles to our own YouTube channels through the YouTube Data API. It is not offered to the public.
